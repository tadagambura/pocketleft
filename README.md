# PocketLeft

**What can I safely spend before payday?**

> #6 of 33 · Wave 1: Daily keepers · Used: Daily · Easy · Revenue potential: High · Money & Work

## What it is

Enter your income dates, bills and savings target, and PocketLeft tells you what you can safely spend today, this week and until payday. Ask 'can I afford £60 tonight?' and get a straight answer.

## Why it's in the portfolio

It answers a question people have every single day, which is the strongest retention driver there is, and it's easier to sell than a full budgeting app because the promise is immediate. It combines three overlapping ideas (ChatGPT's PocketLeft and Payday, Perplexity's Payday Plan) into one stronger app.

## Who it's for

Young workers, students and parents paid weekly, fortnightly, monthly or irregularly, especially anyone who runs short before payday.

**The problem:** Your bank balance isn't what you can spend: rent, bills and savings are still to come out.

## Version one

- Income schedule: weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly, monthly or irregular
- Fixed bills and a savings target
- Safe-to-spend today, this week and until payday
- 'Can I afford £X?' check
- Payday countdown and upcoming-bill alerts

**Example:** Before a £45 night out, a user checks and sees it still leaves enough for rent, travel and food before Friday's payday.

## AI

**Light.** Natural-language 'can I afford it?' ('Can I do £60 tonight and £30 on Saturday?'). Same rule: AI understands the question, code does the maths.

## Watch-outs

The money maths must be correct and clearly not financial advice. The separate Payday repo is retired into this one.
