# PocketLeft

> **Merged into [StackUp](https://github.com/tadagambura/stackup).**

Safe-to-spend until payday, the "can I afford it?" check and subscription reminders now live in StackUp,
next to savings challenges and the daily money lesson. It's the same person at the same moment: someone
counting the days to payday is exactly who wants to stack a little of what's left. Together they make one
money app worth paying for, instead of two that are easy to replace with free tools.

Decided in the November 2026 portfolio review. This repo is kept for history and is no longer developed.
