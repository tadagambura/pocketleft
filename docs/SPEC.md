# PocketLeft: Spec

**Rank in portfolio:** #9 of 15 (easiest → hardest) · **Difficulty:** Easy

## One-liner

What can I safely spend today, this week or until payday?

## Problem / audience

- **Who it's for:** People paid monthly who run out before payday.
- **The problem it solves:** Knowing your balance isn't the same as knowing what you can spend after bills and saving.

## MVP features (ship with only these)

1. Enter income, payday, fixed bills, savings target
2. Safe-to-spend today / this week / until payday
3. Log spending, number updates
4. Payday countdown

## Not in MVP

- Recurring bills calendar
- Payday forecasts
- Multiple budgets

## Monetisation

| Model | Detail |
|---|---|
| Free | Core safe-to-spend, with ads |
| Paid | £2.99/month: recurring bills, forecasts, multiple budgets |
| Ads | Light |

Prices are starting points from the portfolio brief; test before committing.

## Platform & stack

- **Platform:** Expo.
- **Stack:** shared portfolio foundation (Expo, PostHog, RevenueCat, AdMob). See the portfolio roadmap.
- **Data:** Device only. No bank connection.

## Growth engine hooks

- **Shareable output:** Limited: money is private. Lean on SEO and educational content instead.
- **Content pillars:** Payday-to-payday budgeting; Spending money mistakes; Simple budgeting methods

## Notes

**Overlaps heavily with Payday.** Recommend merging Payday's features into PocketLeft. Money maths must be correct and clearly not financial advice.
